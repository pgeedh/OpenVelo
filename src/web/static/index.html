How it works

Three steps, no fitter's studio

  1. Step 1

    Tell us how you ride

    Pick your discipline, flexibility and any discomfort. Targets adapt to you, not a textbook average.

  2. Step 2

    Film 30 seconds of pedalling

    Phone side-on at hip height, bike on a trainer. A live check confirms your framing before you record.

  3. Step 3

    Get your adjustment plan

    Angles with confidence ranges, a ranked list of changes and a saddle-height simulator. Export it as a PDF.

Under the hood

Measured, not guessed

33-point pose tracking

MediaPipe Pose Heavy with heel and toe landmarks, running locally. RTMPose is available as a second engine.

Leg-swap correction

A Viterbi tracker keeps the near and far leg apart, the most common failure of side-on pose models.

Crank-angle modelling

Every frame is placed on the crank circle and each joint is fitted as a smooth curve across all strokes.

Confidence you can see

95% intervals from resampling whole strokes, plus a capture grade so you know when to re-film.

Set up your camera

Good footage is most of a good fit. This takes two minutes.

  • Exactly side-on

    The two wheels should look like circles, not ovals. Film the side you want measured.

  • Whole bike and rider in frame

    Leave a little space around the head and both wheels.

  • Bright, even light

    Avoid a window behind you. Fitted clothing helps the tracker find your joints.

  • Ride normally for 30 seconds

    Hands where you usually ride, around 85–95 rpm at a comfortable effort.

Add your video

MP4, MOV or WebM, 10–60 seconds. A side-on photo also works for a quick static check.
